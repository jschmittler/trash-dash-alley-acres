Visual acceptance checks
  • No opaque pixels touch a cell edge.
  • Feet share one baseline across grounded frames.
  • Head height and body mass stay consistent within each form.
  • Small and large forms preserve the same face, scarf, belt, and tail pattern.
  • Facing flips around the optical center without horizontal jumping.
  • Contact-sheet previews are generated automatically for review.
Gameplay acceptance checks
  • Every reachable player state resolves to a valid animation.
  • Tail-swipe hitbox timing matches the active frames.
  • Hurt completes before shrink, checkpoint reset, or paw loss.
  • Pits still bypass hurt and resolve immediately.
  • Rapid input cannot restart committed sequences.
  • Desktop and mobile rendering use the same state manifest.

Incremental delivery

Checkpoint 1: canonical atlas, manifest, and contact sheet. Checkpoint 2: locomotion and air states in-game. Checkpoint 3: tail swipe, hurt, shrink, glide, victory, and defeat. Checkpoint 4: full local playtest and polish pass.
