How should the approved enemy cast become gameplay sprites?

All ten designs stay recognizable. The decision is how aggressively we simplify them for 64–94px gameplay rendering.

Current visual language
Approved character direction

A — Trashpunk Distillation · Recommended

Preserve every signature prop, costume, silhouette, attitude, and attack. Rebuild with hard dark contours, 3–4 shade ramps, restrained texture, and nearest-neighbor pixels sized for gameplay readability.

B — Literal Illustrated Miniatures

Retain almost all painted texture and surface detail while shrinking the approved sheets. Closest to the source artwork, but busiest at 64–94px and most likely to produce muddy edges or inconsistent animation silhouettes.

C — Current-Level Minimalism

Reduce the new cast to very simple silhouettes and broad color blocks matching today’s Level 1 atlas. Maximum clarity, but it sacrifices much of the fresh personality, scavenged gear, and species-specific storytelling you approved.
