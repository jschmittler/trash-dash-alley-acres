Approved sheets interpreted through the Trashpunk Distillation system. Review identity, silhouette, gear, and attack language—not final pixel cleanup yet.

Level 1 · teaches the combat language

Bottleback Spider

Low, broad purple silhouette carrying a patched green bottle-sack. Curious eyes become predatory during a web-lunge/venom-bite attack. Web strands and impact splashes remain separate FX.

Pot-Helmet Pigeon

Small grounded bruiser with an oversized saucepan helmet, iridescent neck, and permanently annoyed expression. Attacks with a peck/wing flare and committed helmet-first slam.

Needle Mosquito

Small dark-brown flyer with riveted scrap cap, stark pale wings, and a bright red proboscis. Hovers nervously, darts flat and fast, then commits to a precise needle thrust.

Pilfer Opossum

Largest Level 1 regular: hunched thief silhouette, black beanie, purple hoodie, long pink tail, and an overstuffed trash sack. Swipes, lunges, and scatters stolen junk when hit.

Can-Collar Snake

Low olive coil with a battered metal collar and litter caught in its route. Alternates readable idle coils, slither, low dash, tall strike anticipation, and a fast venomous snap.

Level 2 · combines specialized threats

Acorn Squire

Compact orange ranged fighter with saucepan helmet, leaf plume, shoulder armor, and green scarf. Its complete readable performance culminates in a hand-anchored spinning acorn projectile.

Trash-Day Terrier

Largest ordinary Level 2 threat: scarred tan street dog wearing patched scrap armor and a leafy collar. Strong four-legged silhouette, dust-heavy charge, bite/lunge, distinct hit, and readable recovery.

Stinkpunk Skunk

Medium ground controller with huge readable tail, scavenged purple gear, and a portable trash-can nozzle. Tail-rise telegraph leads into a directional gas blast with separate clouds.

Dustwing Moth

Mystical purple flyer with enormous cream wings, bright amber eyes, feathered antennae, and a dangling toxic lantern. Orbits lights, dashes, then swings or releases lantern energy.

Rivet Bee

Fast black-and-gold aerial duelist with riveted armor, aggressive eyes, translucent wings, and a mechanical venom stinger. Readable hover, dash lane, thrust, and venom impact.

Cast rule
One animal, one silhouette, one signature scavenged prop, one unmistakable attack. Repeated trashpunk materials unify the roster; pose, palette, body shape, and attack keep every enemy instantly distinguishable.
