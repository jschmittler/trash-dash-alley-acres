# Decorative side-on source art

These source sheets are the canonical art inputs for the decorative-asset pass.

## Files

- `source/decorative-props-key.png` — 3×2 contact sheet in this order: bush, tree, recycling bin / crate, trail sign, tires.
- `source/branch-platform-key.png` — horizontally tileable grass-and-soil strip.
- `source/metal-platform-key.png` — horizontally tileable riveted metal strip.
- `source/decorative-contact-sheet.png` — review sheet containing all three sources.

## Acceptance checklist

- Strict side-on orthographic viewpoint; no perspective or top faces.
- Flat `#00ff00` chroma-key background with no gradient or shadow outside the art.
- Every prop is fully visible, padded, and grounded to a shared baseline.
- The branch and metal strips have compatible left/right edges for seamless tiling.
- Palette and pixel treatment match the Alley Acres 2-D side-scroller.
