## Power-ups
- Taco Power remains the super-power collectible.
- Kite Power remains the flight collectible.
- Approved concept and sprite sheet live under `reference/items/`.

## Splash screens
Use the approved sprite sheets under `reference/ui-powerups/sprites/`.
- Taco Power approved direction: Taco Fiesta Burst
- Kite Power approved direction: Wind Whirlwind

## End reward
The same dumpster is the end goal in every level.
- Before boss defeat: dull or greyed out.
- After boss defeat: glowing trophy/reward state.
Use `reference/rewards/` as the source of truth.
