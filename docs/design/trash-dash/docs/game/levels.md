# Trash Dash Level Guide

This is the canonical environment progression for Trash Dash. Each level has five environment beats. Concept art lives under `reference/environments/` and should be treated as visual-direction source material for level building, not as literal runtime backgrounds.

## Shared 2.5D environment rule

All level art should support a 2.5D side-scrolling composition with a readable **ground/gameplay plane**, **close background**, **medium background**, and **distant background**. The concept art does not need to be a literal side-view game screenshot. Its job is to establish material language, lighting, depth, landmarks, traversal opportunities, and connected environmental progression.

## Level 1: Woodlands to City Limits

1. Deep woodland starting area with roots, stumps, boulders, hollow logs, and an abandoned campsite.
2. Creek and ruined mill crossing with stepping stones, floating logs, waterwheel platforms, and a broken bridge.
3. Forest-edge highway embankment with culverts, guardrails, fencing, drainage pipes, and the first skyline view.
4. Industrial city fringe with rail cars, shipping containers, warehouses, pallets, and drainage channels.
5. Overgrown urban park transitioning into brick buildings and the quiet neighborhood of Level 2.

The light shifts from warm late afternoon in the forest toward moonlit streets as the raccoon enters civilization.

## Level 2: Suburban After Dark

1. Moonlit backyard with fences, shed, trees, and a trash-can route.
2. Garbage-night street with bins, cars, mailboxes, and wet pavement.
3. Backyard playground with trampoline, pool, sprinklers, treehouse, and fence platforms.
4. Drainage ditch and culvert at the neighborhood edge, with the city beginning to appear beyond.
5. Small suburban main street transitioning into the downtown level.

## Level 3: Downtown Mayhem

1. Restaurant alley with dumpsters, crates, fire escapes, steam vents, and sewer access.
2. Rainy downtown avenue with traffic, scaffolding, awnings, construction barriers, and open manholes.
3. Rooftop run across water towers, antennas, gardens, clotheslines, and suspended walkways.
4. Subway and maintenance tunnels with platforms, trains, electrified rails, ladders, and sewer routes.
5. High-rise construction-site finale with cranes, girders, suspended materials, and a research tower foreshadowing Level 4.

The level has a deliberate vertical rhythm: alley floor, street, rooftops, underground, then the tallest point in the city.

## Level 4: Secret Space Center

1. Hidden storm-drain entrance transitioning from damp city infrastructure into a sterile facility.
2. Experimental laboratory with glowing specimens, containment tanks, robotic arms, and catwalks.
3. Robotics assembly chamber with conveyors, cranes, presses, patrol robots, and laser-testing grids.
4. Zero-gravity research chamber with floating debris, rotating rings, magnetic rails, and air currents.
5. Rocket-hangar finale with scaffolding, fueling equipment, service elevators, and an imminent accidental launch.

The progression begins grounded and claustrophobic, becomes increasingly technologically unbelievable, then reveals the rocket that carries the hero into Level 5.

## Level 5: Raccoon in Space

1. Rocket exterior in low Earth orbit with solar arrays, maintenance hatches, thrusters, and drifting cargo.
2. Satellite graveyard made from rotating dishes, broken capsules, solar panels, and orbital debris.
3. Nebula asteroid mine with crystals, drilling equipment, mine carts, gravity pockets, and crumbling rocks.
4. Abandoned alien station with energy bridges, gravity elevators, portals, and enormous rotating structures.
5. Intergalactic junk-world finale culminating in a glowing trash-can monument at the summit.

## Secret Level 6: The Abandoned Ballpark

**Access method is intentionally TBD.** Do not make this level part of normal progression until the unlock path is explicitly designed.

The level takes place in a fictional, classic North Side Chicago-style baseball stadium in the distant future after humans have disappeared. It should evoke the intimate brick-and-ivy character of an old neighborhood ballpark without copying a real stadium literally. The scoreboard is partially collapsed, seats and concourses are decaying, trash remains everywhere, the grass has gone wild, and fresh ivy has reclaimed the outfield brick wall.

1. **Entryway** - an abandoned brick-and-steel stadium entrance with old gates, overgrowth, damaged signage, litter, and glimpses of the ruined park beyond.
2. **Concessions** - long interior concourse hallways lined with empty, run-down concession stands and bars, broken counters, abandoned cups, boxes, cans, and trash everywhere.
3. **Stadium seats** - traversal through partially broken seating sections with litter underfoot, damaged railings and aisles, and the field visible in the medium and distant background.
4. **Outfield** - the signature ivy-covered brick wall, missing and fallen bricks, warning-track dirt, overgrown grass, bleachers and scoreboard beyond.
5. **Infield / boss arena** - scattered infield dirt and overgrown grass, home plate clearly readable, dugouts in the close-to-medium background, ruined seating and scoreboard farther back. The Level 6 boss fight takes place here.
