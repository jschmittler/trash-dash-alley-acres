# Reusable Trash Dash Foreground Gameplay Asset Prompt

Create one **foreground gameplay asset concept sheet** for `[LEVEL NUMBER + NAME]`, matching its approved environment and character art.

Generate roughly **12-16 modular ground-level props** that make the level feel inhabited and also create gameplay opportunities. Mix small clutter, medium obstacles, standable objects, climbable shapes, breakable props, pushable props, interactive devices, and a few environmental hazards appropriate to the level.

Every object must:
- have a clear grounded base/contact point,
- preserve a readable silhouette at gameplay scale,
- be shown as an isolated asset on a clean presentation background,
- have a believable top surface when it is intended to be standable,
- avoid unexplained floating pieces,
- use the level's established materials, grime, vegetation, lighting accents, and trash language,
- be suitable for a 2.5D side-scrolling gameplay plane.

Label the items and identify their intended gameplay category where useful, such as `standable`, `obstacle`, `breakable`, `pushable`, `interactive`, `hazard`, or `decorative clutter`.

Favor assets that can be repeated, stacked, tiled, or combined without looking identical. Do not bake large background scenery into the objects. Include a small scale/readability guide if space permits.
